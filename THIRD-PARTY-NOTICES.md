# Source attribution and reuse

This repository has no blanket licence, and nothing here overrides the licence of a source document. Copies keep their embedded notices. `benchmark.json` records, for every upstream file, the repository, the commit and the licence statement that was found. No source owner endorses this benchmark or the curator's reading of its documents.

ISA Open Metadata Licence v1.1, stated on the rights page of each PDF and, for GeoDCAT-AP, in the upstream release note. The README of the StatDCAT-AP repository states CC BY 4.0 for its material unless mentioned otherwise.
- **mobilityDCAT-AP 1.0.0**: © 2023 NAPCORE. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), stated in the specification and in the release note.
- **HealthDCAT-AP pilot draft** (state of 20 September 2024): © 2024 European Union; the page states CC BY 4.0 for all material of its repository unless mentioned otherwise.
- **DCAT-AP-JRC Version 1**: © 2014-2019 European Union. CC BY 4.0, stated in the specification; the repository's `LICENCE.md` is the CC BY 4.0 text.

## Bases

- **DCAT-AP 1.1**: © 2015 European Union. ISA Open Metadata Licence v1.1 (rights page of the PDF; `dct:license` of the shapes files).
- **DCAT-AP 2.0.1**: European Union. The PDF states no licence. The README of the `SEMICeu/DCAT-AP` repository states CC BY 4.0 for all its material unless explicitly mentioned otherwise.
- **DCAT-AP 3.0.0**: European Union. CC BY 4.0, stated in the specification.
- **RQ1 base registries** (`bases/rq1-registries/`): built by the RQ1 workbench from the DCAT-AP releases above and from the archived final PDF of DCAT-AP 1.1 (© 2015 European Union, ISA Open Metadata Licence v1.1), which is stored with them. Labels and usage notes in the registries are taken from those specifications.
